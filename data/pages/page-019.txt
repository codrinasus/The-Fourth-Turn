8 EXPLAINABLE-BY-ARCHITECTURE MODELS

We refer to the first family of IBD models as explainable-by-architecture models. Those models can be viewed as a modular framework of multiple components (see Figure 9). The general architecture of these models involves intermediate feature extraction (that might involve feature attributions), and a task-specific decision structure (that might involve feature interactions). Pragmatically speaking, not all components are fully interpretable to ensure competitive task performance.
